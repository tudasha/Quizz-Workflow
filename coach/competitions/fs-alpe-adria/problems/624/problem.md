# Problem 624

You are the last car to start in the Endurance event. So far, the overall fastest time on the time board is 1300 seconds. While watching the event you also saw another team finish endurance in respectable time. You know that team has an Energy Storage that holds only 4,2 kWh.\n From simulations, you know your power consumption depends on the average speed in one lap. The faster the lap, the more energy it consumes.\n EN= P*v^4\n Where: EN is the energy consumed for the whole of Endurance, v is the average speed in km/h, P= 0,0005 [Wh^5/km^4]\n Your Energy Storage holds 6 kWh of energy, and your driver is capable of driving it as fast as possible. BUT! You want to get as many overall points as possible. That means you want to maximize the sum of Endurance event AND Efficiency event points!\n What lap times shall the drivers drive to maximize the points total for both events? The Endurance lap is 1 km long and there are 22 laps in total. Write the answer in seconds with 2 decimal points.\n There is no regeneration of energy. No cones are hit.
