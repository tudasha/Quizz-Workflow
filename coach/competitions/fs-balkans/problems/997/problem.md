# Problem 997

Scenario: The Hockenheimring Midnight Relay

Context:
It is 11:00 PM at the campsite. The grid has failed, and your team of 20 members is starving. You have one electric grill with a nominal power of 2.5 kW. Due to its size, you must cook in shifts for a total duration of 235 minutes. You decide to power the grill using your competition car’s accumulator containers (EV).

Technical Data:
* Accumulator: Each pack has a total capacity of 6.5 kWh and a nominal voltage of 400 VDC.
* Safety Restriction: To prevent overheating of the temporary wiring, officials limit the current draw to a maximum of 10 A per car.
* Inverter: The DC/AC conversion efficiency is 90%.
* Grill Efficiency: The thermal efficiency of the heating element is 70%.

Question:
Based on the Formula Student Rules regarding accumulator protection and the constraints above, what is the minimum number of cars (accumulator containers) required to finish cooking for the entire team without violating any safety or technical limits?

Options:
- A) 2
- B) 3
- C) 4
- D) 5
