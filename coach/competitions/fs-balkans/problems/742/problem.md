# Problem 742

Before the endurance it rained quite badly, the track conditions were set to wet, after the endurance started and the cars started to spread the water, later the track conditions changed to damp. Later it started to rain again, and the conditions of the track changed to wet, and after a while the rain stopped and the track started to dry slowly. So, your team started the endurance event with wet tires on a damp track. The track surface is drying fast, and your telemetry shows that the tires are overheating. The officials did not announce any changes in the track condition.What can you do?

Options:
- A) Make a separate pit stop in the driver change area and change to a new set of wet tires for the remaining laps. The pit stop time is added to the overall time.
- B) You may change to dry tires during the driver change. The pit stop time is not added to overall time if you manage to get it under 13 minutes.
- C) You must not change the tires. You can try driving off the ideal race line within the track boundaries to cool down the tires.
- D) Make a separate pit stop in the driver change area and change to dry tires. The pit stop time is added to the overall time.
