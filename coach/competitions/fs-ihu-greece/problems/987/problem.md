# Problem 987

A 500mm long simply supported panel is loaded at the mid span with moment of 3000 Nm. The panel is made from carbon (outer/inners skins) and aluminum honeycomb (core), like a sandwich structure, placed in your monocoque. To be compliant with the rules and SES you need to calculate the maximum bending stress. The overall depth of the panel is 23mm, the width is 275mm and the thickness of the outer and the inner skins is 1,5mm. Given, also, the elastic modulus of 100GPa, find the stiffness of the panel. (4 points)

Options:
- A) σmax=181 MPa | K=3.667 N/mm
- B) σmax=361 MPa | K=3.667 N/mm
- C) σmax=181 N/mm | K=3.667 MPa
- D) None, because in the 3-point test you are measuring the force applied not the moment.
