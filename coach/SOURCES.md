# Sources

## FS Quiz
Past quizzes, questions, answers, solutions and images come from [FS Quiz](https://fs-quiz.eu) through its API (`https://api.fs-quiz.eu/2/`).
The FS Quiz database is made available under the [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). The problems in `competitions/*/problems/` are a derived database: they stay under ODbL and must keep this credit when shared.

## Rules and handbooks
FS Rules and each competition's handbook belong to their organisers. They are included unchanged (PDF) and converted to Markdown for training only. The official versions on the organisers' websites always win.
